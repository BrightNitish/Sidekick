# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach‑E – Fleet Comparison

| Feature | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach‑E |
|---------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Price (New, 2024)** | $54,990 – $69,990 | $37,995 – $55,995 |
| **EPA Range** | 326 mi (Standard‑Range RWD), 330 mi (Long Range AWD) | 240–310 mi (Standard‑Range RWD 260 mi / Extended‑Range AWD 300 mi) |
| **Battery** | 60 kWh (Std‑R) / 75 kWh (LR/Perf) | 73 kWh (Std‑R) / 88 kWh (Ext‑R) |
| **Fast Charge** | up to 250 kW, ~180 mi in 20 min | 150 kW, ~200 mi in 45 min |
| **Typical Depot Charge** | 45 kW home/work, ~8 h full | 240 V home, 10–15 h full |
| **Maintenance (Annual)** | $600–$1,200 (tire, brake, misc.) | $700–$900 (service, diagnostics) |
| **Cargo Space** | 68 ft³ (rear seats) / 90 ft³ (folded) | 30 ft³ (rear) / 59.7 ft³ (folded) |
| **Telematics + Fleet Tools** | Built‑in V2G, OTA updates, FleetOps API | FordSync™ fleet integration, OTA |
| **Suitability for Sales Fleet** | Excellent city/private‑sales support, low total‑cost of ownership, high cargo, quick charge | Strong brand appeal, competitive price, solid range for suburban use, capable towing up to 2,000 lb |

## Recommendation
For a 100‑car sales fleet focused on city‑centric delivery and showroom support, the **Tesla Model Y** offers the lower operating cost, higher cargo capacity, and faster charging that fit frequent depots. Its premium brand may be a pro for high‑visibility sales roles, but the Mustang Mach‑E delivers competitive economics for a budget‑conscious purchase and offers brand recognition that can be leveraged in retail. **Recommendation:** Deploy the Tesla Model Y as the primary fleet vehicle for 80 vehicles, and use the Ford Mustang Mach‑E for the remaining 20 vehicles where price sensitivity or specific towing needs apply.
