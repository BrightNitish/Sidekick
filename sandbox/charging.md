# Public EV Charging Landscape in the United States

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Total public charging ports (estimated 2024‑25) | **≈ 200,000** | [U.S. Energy & Transportation](https://www.energy.gov/eere/electricvehicles/EV%20charging%20infrastructure)

## Major Charging Networks

### Tesla Supercharger
- **Reach:** 33,400+ ports nationwide, expanding rapidly along highway corridors.
- **Speed:** DC fast charging up to 250 kW (model‑specific). 
- **Reliability:** Consistently high uptime due to closed‑loop fleet management and proprietary software.
- **Why It Matters for a Fleet:** Autonomous routing, real‑time status updates, and integrated payment streamline operations.

### Electrify America
- **Reach:** ~5,100 ports, with a strong focus on inter‑state travel corridors.
- **Speed:** DC fast chargers ranging 50–150 kW.
- **Reliability:** Third‑party audits and comprehensive maintenance contracts.
- **Why It Matters for a Fleet:** Broad coverage across major interstates, tiered pricing plans, and compatibility with most EV makes.

> **Bottom line:** With roughly 200,000 public ports across the country, a U.S.‑based electric fleet can rely on Tesla’s Supercharger network for quick‑stop solutions and Electrify America for longer‑distance planning.
